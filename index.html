About Me

It’s me, Mouad Kacimi. I was born in 2005 in a small village called Rissani, though I prefer to call it Sijilmassa, its historical name. Unlike Rissani, which lacks deep historical meaning, Sijilmassa holds significant historical value. I was born on a Friday, the moment when everyone was gathered at the mosque for prayer. That was the start of my life’s journey. I strongly believe that my existence is not random—I feel that I am meant to make a difference, both personally and socially. Looking back, I can confidently say that I have taken one of the best academic paths among my peers, especially by taking on the challenge of preparatory classes, an experience worth discussing. These intense years have shaped me significantly.
